Privacy Policy for Tablize

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Tablize ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. We built Tablize so that all table detection, list extraction, filtering, and file exports run 100% locally inside your browser.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell the contents of any webpage you visit.
  • We do not track your browsing history, URLs visited, or extracted table data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or telemetry beacons inside the extension.

2. Permissions Used & Why

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click the Tablize toolbar icon to scan the currently active webpage for tables and repeating lists, highlight them on screen, or run the Smart Visual Picker.
  • storage: Used locally on your device (chrome.storage.local) to remember your Pro license status and temporarily hold a list captured by Smart Visual Picker on the current tab.
  • Host permission (https://api.gumroad.com/*): Used solely when you voluntarily enter a Tablize Pro license key and click "Activate", in order to verify key validity with Gumroad's license API.

3. Third-Party Payment & Licensing

If you choose to purchase Tablize Pro, checkout and license key delivery are handled securely by Gumroad. We never see or store your credit card details. When you activate your key inside Tablize, only the license key and product ID are sent to Gumroad's verification endpoint (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify).

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support email listed on the Tablize Chrome Web Store listing or Gumroad receipt.
